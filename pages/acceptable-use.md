## Not permitted

- Generating child sexual abuse material, or any sexual content involving minors.
- Targeted harassment, stalking, doxxing, or credible threats against a person.
- Fraud, phishing, impersonation of a real person or organisation, or synthetic media
  presented as genuine evidence.
- Malware, ransomware, or intrusion tooling aimed at systems you are not authorised to test.
- Content that materially assists the development of weapons capable of mass casualties.
- Circumventing a model host's own policies, or reselling access in breach of their terms.
- Automated evasion of rate limits, quotas or screening — including minting keys to get
  around an [Open Tier](/docs/open-tier) allowance.

## Enforcement

We act on host reports, screening hits, payment disputes and abuse reports. We do not
proactively read your prompts — we cannot; they are not retained.

A breach results in suspension with
[`account_suspended`](/docs/errors#account_suspended). Suspension freezes the balance rather
than confiscating it, and the reply names the reason. Serious cases are reported to the
relevant authority.

## Reporting

Send abuse reports to [support](/support). Include the receipt id if you have one.
